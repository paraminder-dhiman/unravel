UNRAVEL · Torn cloth and frayed threads for Blender
====================================================

QUICK START
1. Select a cloth mesh. It needs UVs for the threads to follow the weave.
2. Right-click in the viewport and choose Make Unravel
   (or use the Unravel tab in the N sidebar).
3. Click Paint Tears and paint red where the cloth should tear.
4. Click Done Painting. Holes, thick edges and threads appear.
5. Try a look from Presets, then fine-tune in the Unravel tab.


THE UNRAVEL TAB (press N in the viewport)
Scale
  Unravel expects real-world size (1 unit = 1 metre) and sets Scale from
  the scene's Unit Scale. If the cloth is 10 times too big, set Scale
  to 10. Raise it on a correctly sized cloth for chunkier threads.
  
Tears
  Hole Size ........ how far holes eat into the painted area (0 = no holes)
  Edge Noise ....... how ragged the edges are
  Noise Scale ...... size of the ragged bits
  Edge Smoothing ... smooths the stepped outline of each hole
  Detail ........... extra subdivision for finer edges (heavier)
  Thickness ........ how thick the torn edges look
  Lip Width ........ how far the thickness reaches from each hole
  Whole Cloth ...... makes the whole cloth solid

Fray Threads and Stray Threads
  Two layers of threads: short dense fray, and long loose strays.
  The checkbox in each title turns that layer on or off.
  Viewport Density . show fewer threads while working; renders show all
  Density, Length .. how many threads, and how long
  Clumping, Curl, Frizz, Droop ... the character of the threads
  Surface Contact .. keeps threads from sinking into the cloth

Thread Material
  Take Colour from Cloth: threads use the cloth's own material, coloured
  by where each thread grows. Untick it to pick your own colour.

Strands
  Hand-drawn loose threads. Use Add Strand (or Shift+A > Unravel Strand),
  draw on the cloth, then press Tab when done.


ANIMATION AND SIMULATION
  Before you simulate or rig the cloth, click Bake Tears (bottom of the
  Tears panel). This cuts the holes into the mesh, so the simulation sees
  them and the cloth flaps around its tears. Thickness, threads and
  strands stay live and follow the cloth. Unbake Tears brings the live
  tears back so you can paint and tweak them again; bake again afterwards.
  Draw strands while the cloth is in its resting shape (frame 1).


TIPS
  · Every Seed has a refresh button for a quick new variation.
  · Presets only change the look. Your paint is never touched.
  · Everything is undoable with Ctrl+Z.
  · All Unravel actions are also under Object > Unravel and in F3 search.


TROUBLESHOOTING
  No threads appear
    · Paint some red: threads only grow along torn edges.
    · Check the cloth has UVs (the panel warns you if not).
    · Check Viewport Density is above 0.
  Threads look white
    · Switch the viewport to Rendered (Z > Rendered).
  The viewport is slow
    · Lower Viewport Density, Detail, or Resolution.
